# Pitch voiceover: read one block per slide (~2 min 30 s total)

Speak slowly and naturally. Urdu-accented English is perfect; judges want to hear the founder.

**Slide 1: Kamai** (15 s)
"Salaam, I'm Nasir from Pakistan, and this is Kamai: escrow payment links that get Pakistani freelancers paid in USDC in seconds."

**Slide 2: The problem** (30 s)
"Pakistan has one of the biggest freelance workforces in the world, but getting paid is still the hardest part. Payoneer and banks take two to three percent and several days. And with direct clients from WhatsApp or Facebook, scams are common: the client takes the work and disappears, or sends a fake 'payment sent' screenshot. Clients don't want to pay first, and freelancers can't afford to work first."
*(If you have a real experience of this, say it here in one sentence. Don't make one up.)*

**Slide 3: The solution** (25 s)
"With Kamai, the freelancer sends a payment link on WhatsApp. The client pays USDC into an escrow locked by a Solana program, not to a person. As each milestone is delivered, the client releases part of the money, and it arrives in under a second for less than a rupee. If the work isn't delivered, the client gets back whatever hasn't been released."

**Slide 4: Proof, not screenshots** (20 s)
"Every escrow has a public proof link that reads straight from the blockchain. Anyone can open it, even without a wallet, and see whether the money is really locked. A fake screenshot can't beat that."

**Slide 5: Built for Pakistan** (15 s)
"The whole app works in Urdu and English, shares invoices on WhatsApp in one tap, and shows every amount in rupees."

**Slide 6: Why Solana** (20 s)
"This only works on Solana. Sub-second finality and tiny fees make escrow possible even for a twenty-dollar gig, and USDC gives freelancers dollar earnings without a bank in the middle. The Anchor program is tested end to end and live on devnet."

**Slide 7: Business & next steps** (20 s)
"Kamai is free today; later we'll charge a small fee on release, still far cheaper than today. Next: cashing out to PKR through licensed local partners, a WhatsApp invoice bot, and on-chain reputation for freelancers. Kamai means 'earnings', and we want every Pakistani freelancer to keep all of theirs. Thank you."
