## LinkedIn (English, professional)

🚀 I just submitted **Kamai** to Colosseum's Crypto World's Fair (Superteam Pakistan Track).

As a Pakistani developer who works with overseas clients, I know the two biggest pains of freelancing:
💸 losing 2–3% and several days to Payoneer and bank transfers
🎭 clients who disappear after delivery, or send fake "payment sent" screenshots

Kamai is a payment link with an escrow built in, on Solana:
✅ The client pays USDC into an escrow locked by a smart contract, not to a person
✅ The client releases it per milestone, and it arrives in under a second for less than 1 rupee
✅ Anyone can verify the payment on-chain, so a fake screenshot can't beat that
✅ Full Urdu interface + WhatsApp sharing
